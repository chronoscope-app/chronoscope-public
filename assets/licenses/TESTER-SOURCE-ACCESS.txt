Chronoscope tester source access

Exact application and dependency source archives accompany each published
executable at its matching release on:
https://github.com/chronoscope-app/chronoscope-public/releases

Choose the release for the downloaded app's version and platform. The latest
source on main may differ from a published executable; use that executable's
release manifest and source archives for its exact build revision.

Download chronoscope-source.zip (complete application/local crates/patched GLib)
and chronoscope-dependency-sources.tar.xz (exact checksum-verified Cargo and npm
registry archives with original license/copyright/NOTICE files). The release's
checksum and source/release manifests identify the matching revisions. Extract .crate/.tgz
archives with tar. MPL-covered files remain under MPL-2.0; their exact sources
are provided in the dependency archive, including conservative extra platform
packages. Apache-2.0 alternatives are selected where offered; combined/AND
obligations and independent data/media terms remain applicable.

The application source includes frontend/vite.config.ts modification replacing
Knockout global eval lookup with globalThis and vendor/glib/BACKPORT.md.
Build/replacement commands and exact toolchain are in .github/workflows/build.yml
and docs/DISTRIBUTION.md. You may modify covered libraries and reverse-engineer
for debugging those modifications to the extent required by their licenses.
Linux native source/replacement records are separately required for Linux
redistribution; registry archives do not stand in for distro source packages.

No executable may be published until its matching source archives are
attached and anonymously retrievable. Preserve this notice with redistributed
installers/apps, along with all applicable upstream texts.
